## 1. Admin Console Layout & Navigation (`23 Screens`)
- Left navigation grouped into 5 operational pillars:
  1. **Catalog & Pricing Ops** (`Providers`, `SKU Catalog`, `Price Sync Jobs`, `Quarantine & Failures`)
  2. **Affiliate & Revenue Ops** (`Affiliate Accounts`, `Campaigns`, `Redirect Links`, `Commission Ledger`, `CSV/Postback Reconciliation`, `Sponsored Slots`)
  3. **BYOC & Security Ops** (`Connection Monitor`, `Server Operation Audit`, `RBAC Permissions`, `Security Events & Emergency Freeze`, `Zero-Trust Audit Center`)
  4. **Content & SEO Ops** (`Tutorials & Benchmarks CMS`, `Customer Reviews Moderation`, `SEO & Sitemap Governance`)
  5. **Platform & SRE Ops** (`External API Circuit Breakers`, `Asynq Job Queue & DLQ`, `Feature Flags & Settings`, `Deployments & DR Status`)
