## 1. Admin Console Layout & Navigation
- Left navigation grouped into 5 operational pillars:
  1. **Catalog & Deal Ops** (`Vendors`, `Tool & Plan Catalog`, `Deal Sync Jobs`, `Quarantine & Failures`)
  2. **Affiliate & Revenue Ops** (`Affiliate Accounts`, `Campaigns`, `Redirect Links`, `Commission Ledger`, `CSV/Postback Reconciliation`, `Sponsored Slots`)
  3. **Security & Access Ops** (`RBAC Permissions`, `Security Events & Emergency Freeze`, `Zero-Trust Audit Center`)
  4. **Content & SEO Ops** (`Tutorials & Deal Guides CMS`, `Customer Reviews Moderation`, `SEO & Sitemap Governance`)
  5. **Platform & SRE Ops** (`External API Circuit Breakers`, `Asynq Job Queue & DLQ`, `Feature Flags & Settings`, `Deployments & DR Status`)
