# SEARCH_COMPARISON.md — Global Search, Natural Language Bar & Side-by-Side Comparison UX

**Document ID:** `CS-UX-003`

> **Pivot addendum (2026-11-26):** Reframed for TirpScanner — deal/tool search and plan comparison replace cloud-spec search and SKU matrices.

---

## 1. Command-K Natural Language Search Bar
- Typing `ai video editor under $20 with annual discount` renders interactive removable filter chips below the input:
  `[Category: AI Video ×]` `[Max Price <= $20/mo ×]` `[Billing: Annual ×]` `[Has Trial ×]`
- Users can toggle between **Deal Data-Grid Mode** (dense sortable table: plan, billing cycle, seats, monthly-equivalent, discount, trial, deal expiry) and **Guided Card Mode** (highlighting Value Score, Pros/Cons, and Trial/Deal badges).

## 2. Side-by-Side Multi-Plan Comparison Matrix (`/compare`)
- Supports pinning up to **6 plans** simultaneously across different vendors and native currencies.
- Includes **"Highlight Differences Only"** toggle and **"Winner Per Row"** subtle green badge (lowest monthly-equivalent, highest discount, longest trial, best value score).
