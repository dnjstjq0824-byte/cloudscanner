**Document ID:** `CS-AE-005`
## 1. Step-by-Step Affiliate Registration Guide for Korean Individual Operators
1. **Prerequisites Before Applying**:
   - Deploy CloudScanr live on `https://cloudscanr.com` with at least 10+ original cloud comparison pages, benchmark methodology, and `/affiliate-disclosure` live (networks reject under-construction or blank sites).
   - Prepare domain email (`partners@cloudscanr.com`).
   - Prepare payout accounts: **PayPal** (verified Korean account), **Payoneer** (USD/EUR virtual receiving bank accounts for CJ/Awin), and a Korean Foreign Currency Bank Account (**외화통장** with SWIFT code for Impact/Cloudways wire transfers).
2. **Tax Documentation (US W-8BEN)**:
   - For US networks/merchants (Impact, CJ, DigitalOcean, Vultr, GCP), complete electronic **IRS Form W-8BEN** as a non-US individual resident of the Republic of Korea (entering Korean Resident Registration Number / Tax ID as FTIN).
3. **Strict Anti-Violation Rules**:
   - Zero bidding on provider trademark keywords (`DigitalOcean`, `Cloudways`, `Vultr`, `UpCloud`, `Hetzner`, `Contabo`) on Google Ads / Naver Ads.
   - Zero self-referrals (never click `/go/*` to register CloudScanr's own infrastructure accounts).
   - Zero cookie stuffing, hidden iframes, or forced auto-redirects.
